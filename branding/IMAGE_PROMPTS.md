# Optional image-generation prompts

All brand assets in this folder are **self-made vector art** rendered from code (no image-generation tool was available). If you later want painterly artwork for thumbnails, use these prompts in any image generator. Then drop the image in as the thumbnail `Art` (see README). Always add the Hindi text with the template, never inside the generated image, because generators misspell Devanagari.

Shared style suffix (append to every prompt):

> cinematic 2D illustrated painting, flat layered shapes with subtle paper texture, warm terracotta and charcoal palette with parchment highlights and touches of gold, low golden-hour light, atmospheric dust, documentary mood, no text, no letters, no watermark, 16:9

## Ep01 — Indus Valley city at dusk
> An abandoned ancient Indus Valley (Harappan) city at sunset, seen from a wide brick-paved street. Flat-roofed baked-brick houses with small doorways and covered drains along the street, a raised mud-brick citadel mound on the right with the setting sun just above it, long shadows and drifting dust, a few faint translucent figures suggesting memories of past residents, empty and quiet. Composition: subject on the right half, darker open sky and street on the left for text.

Accuracy notes: there are no temples, domes, arches or columns in Harappan cities, and no horses or chariots. Use baked-brick walls in 1:2:4 proportions, flat roofs, wells and covered drains.

## Template example — Indus script seal
> Close-up of an ancient square steatite stamp seal from the Indus Valley, carved with a one-horned bull ("unicorn") and a short row of undeciphered signs above it, resting on dark earth, a raking warm light from the top right, shallow depth of field, the seal on the right side of frame, dark empty space on the left.
